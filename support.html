Support

Turning it on, using it, and what to do when a highlight doesn’t come back.

Turn it on: Mac

  1. Open the Text Highlighter app once. Its window shows whether the extension is on.
  2. Click Quit and Open Safari Settings…, or in Safari choose Safari › Settings › Extensions.
  3. Tick Text Highlighter, then choose Always Allow on Every Website when Safari asks. (You can allow it on chosen sites only; highlights then work on those sites.)

Turn it on: iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings, then Apps, then Safari, then Extensions.
  2. Tap Text Highlighter and turn on Allow Extension.
  3. Set All Websites to Allow.

Then open any web page, such as the demo page, and select some text.

Use it

  • Highlight: select text and pick a colour in the bar next to it. On a Mac you can also press ⌃⇧H, or right-click and choose Highlight Selection.
  • Change it: click (or tap) a highlight to recolour it, add or edit a note, copy it or remove it.
  • See a page’s highlights: press ⌃⇧L, or click the Text Highlighter toolbar button and choose Show Highlights on This Page. On iPhone and iPad, tap the page menu button beside the address, then Text Highlighter.
  • All highlights: Open Library in the toolbar button lists every page, with search, filters, notes and export.
  • Turn it off for a site: untick Highlight on the site in the toolbar button. Highlights there are kept and come back when you turn it on again.

Common questions

A highlight didn’t come back

If the page changed so much that the words can’t be found, the highlight is kept and listed under Not found in the page’s panel and in the library. Nothing is deleted. If the words appear later (some pages load their text slowly, or as you scroll), the highlight is painted as soon as they do.

The bar doesn’t appear when I select text

Check that Show the toolbar when I select text is ticked in the toolbar button, and that highlighting isn’t turned off for the site. The keyboard shortcut and the right-click menu work either way. Text in form fields can’t be highlighted.

The toolbar button says it can’t see this page

Safari hasn’t allowed the extension on this website. Allow it in Safari’s settings for the extension, then reload the page. Safari doesn’t let extensions into its own pages, such as Settings or the Start Page.

How do I move my highlights to another device?

In the library, choose Backup (JSON) and Export, then on the other device use Import Backup…. Highlights that are already there aren’t duplicated.
