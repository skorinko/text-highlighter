Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

In short: Text Highlighter does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Your highlights and notes stay in Safari on your own device.

Who we are

Text Highlighter for Safari is published by Dmitry Skorinko, an individual developer, through the App Store.

What the extension sees

So that your highlights can reappear when you come back to a page, Text Highlighter runs on the web pages you open, on the websites you allow in Safari. On each page it looks only for the text of your own highlights. It doesn’t keep the page’s text, doesn’t record which pages you visit, and has no access to your browsing history.

You decide where it runs: allow it on every website, or only on the sites you choose, in Safari’s settings; or turn highlighting off for a site from its toolbar button.

What is stored

When you highlight text, the extension stores the highlighted words and a few words around them, your note if you write one, the colour, the date, and the address and title of the page. This is kept in Safari’s storage for the extension, on your device. Your settings, such as the colour for new highlights, are kept there too.

Nothing leaves your device unless you export or copy it yourself. Removing the app removes everything it stored.

Network requests

Text Highlighter makes no requests to the developer or to any third party. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code.

Purchases

Text Highlighter is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and the developer does not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Children

Text Highlighter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
